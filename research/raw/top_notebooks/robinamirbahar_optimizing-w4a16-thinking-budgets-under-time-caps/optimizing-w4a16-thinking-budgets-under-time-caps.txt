# Optimizing W4A16 Thinking Budgets Under Strict Wall-Clock Timeouts
This notebook analyzes the **LLM W4A16 Quantization & Thinking Budget Bench** (`robinamirbahar/llm-w4a16-quantization-thinking-budget-bench`) for `gemma-4-31b-it-qat-w4a16-ct`.


##### [code 1]
import glob
import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

paths = glob.glob('/kaggle/input/**/w4a16_thinking_budget_latency.csv', recursive=True)
df = pd.read_csv(paths[0])
display(df)


##### [code 2]
fig, ax1 = plt.subplots(figsize=(8, 4.5), dpi=120)
ax1.bar(df['config_id'], df['task_resolution_rate_pct'], color='#2b5c8f', alpha=0.85, label='Resolution Rate (%)')
ax1.set_ylabel('Task Resolution Rate (%)', color='#2b5c8f')
ax1.set_xlabel('Configuration ID (BF16 / W4A16_QAT / FP8)')
ax2 = ax1.twinx()
ax2.plot(df['config_id'], df['mean_turn_latency_sec'], color='#d95f02', marker='o', linewidth=2.5, label='Mean Turn Latency (s)')
ax2.set_ylabel('Mean Turn Latency (sec)', color='#d95f02')
plt.title('Gemma 4 31B: Task Resolution Rate vs Turn Latency Across Quantization Configs')
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()
